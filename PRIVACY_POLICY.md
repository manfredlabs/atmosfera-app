# Privacy Policy - Atmosfera

**Last updated:** April 4, 2026

## Overview

Atmosfera is a music pad app designed for live worship and musical performances. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

Atmosfera **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**. The app works entirely offline on your device.

Specifically, Atmosfera:
- Does not require an internet connection
- Does not collect personal information
- Does not use analytics or tracking
- Does not use cookies
- Does not share data with third parties
- Does not require user registration or login

## Data Stored on Device

The app stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- Song configurations (name, key, BPM, time signature)
- App settings (volume levels, audio channel preferences)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Children's Privacy

Atmosfera does not collect any data from any user, including children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
